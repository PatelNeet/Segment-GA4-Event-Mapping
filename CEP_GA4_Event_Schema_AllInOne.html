Order value metrics — Segment ↔ GA4

How Segment metrics map to GA4 metrics for item-level and order-level values — with definitions, equations and a worked sample (order subtotal €52.89, shipping €24.99, VAT 23%).

Item-Level Metrics

Segment MetricGA4 MetricDefinitionEquationSample Value
list_priceoriginal_priceThe original / undiscounted unit price of the item before any item-level promotion is applied. Maps to original_price in GA4.— (base value)€52.89
priceitem priceThe final unit selling price per item after item-level discount is applied, but before tax & shipping. This is what actually gets charged for the item.price = list_price − discount€52.89 (no discount applied here)
discountitem discountThe item-level discount amount — the difference between what the item would have cost at list price vs. what it actually sold for. Separate from order-level coupon savings.discount = list_price − price€0
tax—The VAT / tax amount attributable to this specific line item, calculated by applying the tax rate to the item's price.tax = price × VAT_rate€52.89 × 23% = €12.16 ✓ matches payload exactly
quantityquantityThe number of line-item units of this product ordered — how many of this SKU / cart-line the customer is buying.— (cart count)1
sales_quantitynumber_of_unitsThe number of individual physical units contained within one line item — e.g. a "pack of 10 mugs" sold as a single line item still contains 10 physical units. Maps to number_of_units in GA4.— (pack size, independent of quantity)10

Order-Level Metrics

Segment MetricGA4 MetricDefinitionEquationSample Value
revenuerevenueThe total value of all items in the order, summed across line items, after item-level discounts but before shipping and tax. Maps to value in GA4 / "Subtotal" in the UI. Used up to the add_shipping_info event.revenue = Σ(item.price × item.quantity)52.89
revenuerevenue + shippingThe total value of all items in the order, summed across line items, after item-level discounts, with shipping added and before tax. Used from the add_shipping_info event up to the purchase event.revenue = Σ(item.price × item.quantity) + shipping(52.89 + 24.99) = 77.88
shippingshippingThe delivery / carriage fee charged for fulfilling the order. Maps to "Delivery" in the UI.— (flat / calculated fee)24.99
discountorder_discountThe order-level discount — the monetary value of any coupon / voucher applied across the whole order (distinct from item-level discount). Maps to "Savings" in the UI; empty order_discount in GA4 when zero.discount = revenue_before_coupon − revenue_after_coupon€0 (coupon 60BIRTHDAY applied but yielded €0 savings)
taxtaxThe total VAT charged on the order, applied to the combined value of goods and shipping (not just goods). Maps to "VAT (23%)" in the UI.tax = VAT_rate × (revenue + shipping − discount)23% × (€52.89 + €24.99) = €17.91 ✓ matches payload exactly
Issue — Tax conflation: confirming that order-level tax isn't just a rollup of item-level tax — it includes tax on shipping too.
Issue — Discount value: discount is available at item level but not at order level (Segment implementation issue; GA4 follows the same issue).
